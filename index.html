From the analyser to the patient's phone

There is no website to log in to and no server in between. The report goes from your computer to your Google Drive, and from there to the patient.

  1. Connect Google Drive

    Click Connect Google Drive once and sign in with the lab's Google account.

  2. Enter results

    Fill in the results as usual. Calculated values like absolute counts, indices and eGFR fill themselves in.

  3. Report is saved to Drive

    When the report is complete, the PDF is uploaded to a "Labsoft GD Reports" folder in your Drive.

  4. Patient scans the QR

    The printed report carries a QR code. Scanning it opens the PDF, ready to download or forward.

Everything a pathology lab needs every day

Labsoft GD runs on your own Windows computer and keeps working without the internet. Drive uploads simply wait until you are back online.

Ready-made test catalogue

CBC with absolute counts, KFT with Urea/BUN ratio and eGFR, LFT, lipid profile, Widal, CHIKV and hundreds more, with reference ranges.

Reports that look like yours

Choose from header designs, use your own letterhead, and add up to three signatures with name, designation and degree.

Billing and receipts

Bills with discounts, part payments and dues, printed on A4 or thermal paper, with patient and doctor-wise summaries.

Same link after every correction

If you edit a report and save it again, the file in Drive is replaced, so the QR code already printed still opens the latest report.

Links that expire

Choose how many days a report stays shared. After that, the link stops working and the file stays private in your Drive.

Share on WhatsApp

Send the report PDF to the patient's WhatsApp straight from the report screen.

Your reports stay in your Drive

Labsoft GD asks Google only for permission to manage the files it creates. It cannot open the rest of your Drive, your email or your contacts.

Read the full privacy policy

  • Nothing comes to us. Reports go from your computer straight to your Google Drive. PathCare Solutions never receives them.
  • Only its own files. The program sees just the "Labsoft GD Reports" folder and the reports inside it.
  • Sign-in stays on your computer. The Google sign-in is stored encrypted on the lab computer, never on a server.
  • You can disconnect any time, from Labsoft GD or from your Google Account settings.

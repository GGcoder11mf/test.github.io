Privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026

In short: Labsoft GD saves the lab's report PDFs into the lab's own Google Drive. It can only see the files it creates there. Nothing is sent to PathCare Solutions, and no data passes through any server of ours, because we do not run one.

Who we are

Labsoft GD is pathology laboratory management software for Microsoft Windows, made by PathCare Solutions, Taramandal, Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh, India ("we", "us"). It is installed and runs on the laboratory's own computer. This policy explains what the software does with Google account data and with the information the laboratory enters.

Google data we use

When a laboratory chooses Connect Google Drive, Labsoft GD asks Google for one permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: to create files and manage only the files that Labsoft GD itself created in the user's Google Drive.

With this permission Labsoft GD cannot see, open, change or delete any other file in the Drive. It does not ask for access to Gmail, contacts, calendar or any other Google service.

Labsoft GD also reads the email address of the connected Google account, through the Google Drive API, only so that the program can show which account reports are being saved to.

How it is used

  • To create a folder named "Labsoft GD Reports" in the laboratory's Drive.
  • To upload each finished report as a PDF into that folder, and to replace that PDF when the laboratory corrects and saves the report again.
  • To turn on a view-only "anyone with the link" sharing setting for that report file, so that the QR code printed on the report can open it.
  • To remove that sharing setting after the number of days the laboratory chooses.

Google data is used only to provide these features to the laboratory. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not used to train any artificial intelligence or machine learning model.

Where it is stored

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the laboratory's computer, in the Windows user profile, encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI). It is never sent to PathCare Solutions.

Patient details, test results, bills and settings are kept in the Labsoft GD data file on the laboratory's computer. Report PDFs are kept in the laboratory's own Google Drive, under the laboratory's Google account and subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

PathCare Solutions has no server for this software and receives no copy of the laboratory's data or reports.

Sharing of reports

A report uploaded by Labsoft GD can be opened by anyone who has its link, for example by scanning the QR code printed on that report. The link is long and random, and it is not listed or searchable anywhere. Each link opens only that one report, never the folder or any other file.

The laboratory decides whether reports are uploaded automatically and for how many days links stay active. After that time Labsoft GD removes the sharing, and the file stays private in the laboratory's Drive. The laboratory can also stop sharing or delete any report directly in Google Drive.

Patient information

The laboratory using Labsoft GD decides what patient information is entered and which reports are shared, and is responsible for that information under the laws that apply to it. PathCare Solutions does not access, collect or process patient information. Laboratories should hand printed reports with QR codes only to the patient or the person the patient has authorised.

Limited Use

Labsoft GD's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and deleting

  • In Labsoft GD, open Settings, Google Drive, and click Disconnect. The sign-in is withdrawn from Google and deleted from the computer.
  • You can also remove Labsoft GD's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Report files stay in your Drive until you delete them. Deleting the "Labsoft GD Reports" folder deletes all of them, and their links stop working.
  • Uninstalling Labsoft GD removes the program; the laboratory's data file can be deleted from the computer by the laboratory.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Labsoft GD uses Google data, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change reaches the software.
